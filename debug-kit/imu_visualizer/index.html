How to connect
  1. Upload imu_visualizer.ino to your ESP32-C6 (wired like step 2).
  2. Close the Arduino Serial Monitor. Only one program can use the USB port.
  3. Click Connect board above and pick the ESP32's COM port.
  4. Keep the board still for one second while it calibrates, then turn it.
  5. Nothing showing up? Press RESET on the board, or check the messages box for a FAIL.
